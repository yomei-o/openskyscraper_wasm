OpenSkyscraper

an open reimplementation of SimTower, compiled to WebAssembly

The game's graphics and sounds live inside the original SIMTOWER.EXE, which is not distributed here. Pick your own copy to play.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere: the file is read in your browser and stays there.

It must be the 16-bit Windows 3.1 release — the graphics are read out of that executable's NE resource table. A Mac copy, or a 32-bit repackaging, will not work.
